# Equity compensation reference (RSU / ESPP / ISO)

## RSUs
- Vest-date FMV is ordinary income (W-2 box 1) and **is the cost basis**. Compare the 1099-B basis: for noncovered lots (Box B) brokers often report only the vest value correctly, but verify against the vest schedule and W-2 box 14.
- Selling at vest creates about zero gain; later appreciation is short-term until held more than one year.
- Tie out: total RSU income = W-2 box 14 RSU line.

## ESPP
- Disqualifying disposition (sold within 2 years of the offering date or 1 year of purchase): the discount (purchase-date FMV minus price paid) is ordinary income, already in W-2 box 1 and box 14 "ESPPD".
- The 1099-B basis usually excludes it, so Form 8949 needs an adjustment: Box A/B/C as reported, **code B**, adjustment = the ordinary income, or the income is taxed twice. Tie out: sum of adjustments = W-2 box 14 ESPPD.
- Qualifying disposition: ordinary income is the lesser of the actual gain or the offering-date discount; the rest is long-term gain. Use Form 3922 for dates and FMVs.

## ISOs
- Regular tax: nothing at exercise. Sold in a qualifying disposition (2 years from grant and 1 year from exercise) = long-term gain over the exercise price. Disqualifying = the spread at exercise is ordinary income (the rest capital).
- **AMT**: the exercise-year spread is an AMT preference when shares are held past year-end (Form 6251). Ask whether any exercise-and-hold happened in a past year. If AMT was paid, a **prior-year minimum tax credit (Form 8801)** may apply in the year the shares are sold; basis for AMT differs from regular basis.
- Need Form 3921 for each exercise. Never assume $0 AMT without a Form 6251 check.

## Wash sales
- A loss sale is disallowed if substantially identical shares are acquired (including RSU vests and ESPP purchases) within 30 days before or after. Add the disallowed loss to the replacement lot's basis; Form 8949 code W.
