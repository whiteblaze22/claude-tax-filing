# Virginia (Form 760) reference

Look up every number each year at tax.virginia.gov (Form 760 instructions). Values below are rules to check, not constants.

## Flow
1. Start from **federal AGI** (Form 760 line 1).
2. Apply additions and subtractions (Schedule ADJ): e.g. out-of-state/other bond interest additions, age deduction, state tax refund subtraction, tax-exempt-interest items. Do not assume zero; ask.
3. Deduction: **compare** the Virginia standard deduction with Virginia itemized deductions and use the larger.
4. Personal exemptions are a **deduction per person** (taxpayer, spouse, dependents), not a credit. Add age/blind exemptions if applicable.
5. Tax from the Virginia rate schedule on the result; subtract credits (e.g. credit for tax paid to another state).
6. Payments: Virginia withholding (W-2 box 17) + estimated payments (Form 760ES) + any extension payment.

## Itemized deductions: known pitfalls
- Virginia itemized deductions start from the federal Schedule A, but **state and local income taxes are not deductible** (they are added back). Do not put Virginia withholding / income tax in the Virginia itemized total. This was the 2025 error.
- Real estate and personal property taxes, mortgage interest and charity are allowed, subject to Virginia's treatment of the federal SALT cap. **Check whether Virginia follows the current federal SALT cap** (conformity date) rather than assuming.
- Personal property tax: use the real bills/receipts. Scanned bills need OCR; never estimate.

## Other checks
- A prior-year **state refund (1099-G)** is taxable on the federal return only if you itemized that year and it produced a benefit; Virginia subtracts it.
- Dependents and exemptions on Form 760 must match the federal return.
- Payment: Virginia Tax online account / Pay with Bank Account; keep the confirmation and confirm it posts. Estimated payments by 12/31 are deductible on the **federal** Schedule A only as part of the SALT total (not on Virginia).
- If Virginia sends a document request after filing (as in 2025), respond with the schedules the return relied on (income schedule, itemized deductions).
